Research

My research is in public-key cryptography, centred on functional encryption: encryption that lets an authorised key holder learn one specific function of the encrypted data, such as an inner product or a quadratic function, and nothing else about it.

I have designed inner-product and quadratic functional encryption schemes with features needed in practice: unbounded vector lengths, predicate and attribute-based access control, multi-client and identity-based settings, and tracing of leaked decryption keys. These constructions use bilinear pairings, pairing-free groups and lattices, with a focus on provable security and short ciphertexts and keys.

More broadly, I work on fully homomorphic encryption and private set intersection, with the common goal of computing on data that stays encrypted, for example in cloud services.
